# ProcureAI — Human-in-the-Loop Decision System (Phase 10)

## Executive Summary

Phase 10 establishes the binding procurement decision workflow for ProcureAI, embodying the foundational governance principle:

> **"The AI recommendation must NEVER automatically award the tender."**  
> **`AI RECOMMENDS · HUMANS DECIDE · SYSTEM AUDITS`**

While artificial intelligence evaluates multi-criteria factor scores, generates explainability profiles, and detects statistical anomalies, the final legal authority to award or reject contracts rests exclusively with authorized government procurement officers.

---

## 1. The 7-Point Officer Decision Dossier

Before taking any binding action, the government officer is presented with a comprehensive 7-point decision dossier:

1. **Eligible Bidders**: Complete roster of companies that passed mandatory eligibility qualification gates.
2. **Bid Values**: Commercial pricing in INR with deviation percentages relative to official estimated budget.
3. **Evaluation Scores**: Composite 0–100 scores and granular criteria-level breakdowns (Price 40%, Technical 20%, Experience 15%, Financial 10%, Performance 10%, Risk 5%).
4. **AI Recommendation**: Identification of the top-ranked proposal (Rank #1) with recommendation type and confidence indicator.
5. **Risk Indicators**: Statistical outlier alerts generated by the Isolation Forest engine (e.g. abnormal dumping, unrealistic delivery compression).
6. **Explainability Report**: Plain-language non-technical qualitative ratings grid (*Excellent*, *Very strong*, *Good*, *Low*), positive contributors (`+`), negative contributors (`-`), and SHAP baseline attribution meter.
7. **Audit Information**: Cryptographic sealed-bid verification status, evaluation timestamp, model version, and immutable audit metadata.

---

## 2. Permitted Actions & Mandatory Validation Controls

### Action 1: `[APPROVE RECOMMENDATION]`
- Approves the top AI-recommended company for contract award.
- Flags `followed_ai = TRUE` (`override_status = 'NO'`).
- Records standard governance approval rationale.
- Computes SHA-256 integrity hash and seals the decision.

### Action 2: `[REJECT RECOMMENDATION]`
- Allows rejecting the AI recommendation to:
  1. *Award an Alternative Bidder*,
  2. *Reject All Bids*,
  3. *Cancel the Procurement Tender*, or
  4. *Defer Decision to Higher Committee*.
- **Mandatory Requirements when Selecting Another Bidder**:
  - **Selected Bidder**: Must specify an eligible bidder from the evaluated pool.
  - **Reason Category**: Must select a valid justification type (`committee_directive`, `additional_information`, `policy_exception`, `emergency`, `ai_error`, `other`).
  - **Detailed Justification**: Substantive written rationale (minimum 50 characters required).
  - **Supporting Note**: Formal documentation reference (minimum 10 characters required, e.g. committee minute ref or file number).
- **Mandatory Requirements when Rejecting All Bids or Cancelling**:
  - Substantive written explanation (minimum 20 characters required).

---

## 3. Cryptographic Chaining & SHA-256 Integrity Hash

Every final procurement decision is cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 over a canonical pipe-delimited string:

$$\text{hash} = \text{SHA256}(\text{officer\_id} \parallel \text{timestamp} \parallel \text{tender\_id} \parallel \text{ai\_rec} \parallel \text{final\_decision} \parallel \text{selected\_bidder} \parallel \text{override\_status} \parallel \text{reason})$$

### Recorded Governance Fields (All 8 Required):
1. `officer ID`
2. `timestamp`
3. `AI recommendation`
4. `final decision`
5. `selected bidder`
6. `override status` (`YES` / `NO`)
7. `reason`
8. `integrity hash`

---

## 4. Pre-Submission Confirmation Screen

To prevent accidental awards or hasty overrides, the officer must complete a two-step confirmation review:
- Displays complete summary of the action, selected entity, override status, recorded reason, and supporting documentation references.
- Previews the cryptographic signature.
- Displays explicit legal disclaimer: *"Submitting this decision will cryptographically seal the procurement outcome and permanently lock the decision record from ordinary modification."*
- Requires officer to click `[Confirm & Cryptographically Lock Decision]`.

---

## 5. Post-Decision Immutability Locking

Once recorded:
1. `is_locked` is set to `TRUE`.
2. Database trigger `trg_lock_government_decisions` prevents any subsequent `UPDATE` or `DELETE` operations on the decision row.
3. Tender transitions to `AWARDED` or `DECISION_MADE`, locking all submitted bids.
4. An immutable event is written to `audit_logs`:
   - Event: `DECISION_FINALIZED_LOCKED`
   - Description with Integrity Hash and tamper-evident payload.
5. Government UI displays permanent 🔒 **OFFICIAL PROCUREMENT DECISION LOCKED & SIGNED** badge with clickable hash verification.
